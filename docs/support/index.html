Support — Picross: Cryptozoology

Picross: Cryptozoology is a single-player nonogram puzzle game: 400 hand-drawn puzzles across five regions, each one hiding a cryptid to uncover.

Contact

Questions, bug reports, and feedback: Codemackcreations@gmail.com

I'm a solo developer and reply within a few business days. Including your device model and iOS version helps a lot with bug reports.

Restoring a purchase

The all-access unlock is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID, so it carries across reinstalls and to your other devices.

  1. Open Settings in the app
  2. Tap Restore Purchases

You can also restore from the unlock screen itself. If a restore doesn't bring back a purchase you've made, email me with the date of purchase and I'll sort it out.

Progress and data

Puzzle progress and settings are stored on your device only — there's no account and no login. Deleting the app deletes that progress, and it can't be recovered. Your purchase is unaffected and can always be restored.
