Picross: Cryptozoology ("the app", "we", "us") is a single-player nonogram puzzle game. We
respect your privacy and keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the game, show
ads to non-paying players, and process purchases.

## What we collect

**We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email, or
contacts.** Your puzzle progress, settings, and purchases are stored **locally on your
device**.

The app uses a small number of third-party services that may collect data:

### Advertising — Google AdMob
For players who have **not** purchased the ad-free unlock, we show interstitial ads via
Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process:
- device and advertising identifiers (e.g. IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android),
- approximate, non-precise usage and device information,
- IP address (used to serve and measure ads).

On iOS you are asked (via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt) whether the app may use
this data to track you for **personalized** ads. If you decline — or where required by law
(e.g. the EEA/UK, handled via Google's consent form) — ads are served in **non-personalized**
mode. Google's handling of this data is governed by the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

### Purchases — RevenueCat + Apple/Google
The one-time "Unlock Everything" purchase is processed by the App Store (Apple) or Google
Play, with entitlements managed by RevenueCat. These services process:
- a purchase/transaction record and receipt,
- an anonymous app-user identifier used to restore your purchase across devices.

We do not receive your payment card details. See the
[RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) and your app store's privacy
terms.

## How we use data
- To display and measure ads for non-paying players.
- To grant and restore the ad-free / all-regions unlock.
- To keep the app working (basic, non-identifying diagnostics).

We do **not** sell your personal data.

## Your choices
- **Remove ads entirely** by purchasing the one-time unlock.
- **Change ad-tracking consent** any time in the app under **Settings → Privacy → Ad Privacy
  Choices**, or in your device's system privacy settings.
- **Delete your data:** uninstalling the app removes all locally stored progress. For any
  data held by Google or RevenueCat, use their respective privacy controls.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal
information from children. Ads are configured for a general audience.
