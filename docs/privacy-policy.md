# Privacy Policy — Picross: Cryptozoology

**Effective date:** 2026-09-21
**Contact:** Codemackcreations@gmail.com
**Developer:** CodeMack

Picross: Cryptozoology ("the app", "we", "us") is a single-player nonogram puzzle game. We
respect your privacy and keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the game and
process purchases.

## What we collect

**We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email, or
contacts.** Your puzzle progress, settings, and purchases are stored **locally on your
device**.

The app uses one third-party service that may collect data:

### Purchases — RevenueCat + Apple

The one-time "Unlock Everything" purchase is processed by the App Store (Apple), with
entitlements managed by RevenueCat. This service processes:
- a purchase/transaction record and receipt,
- a device identifier used to look up and restore your purchase.

We do not receive your payment card details. See the
[RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) and Apple's privacy terms.

## How we use data

- To grant and restore the all-access unlock.

We do **not** sell your personal data.

## Your choices

- **Delete your data:** uninstalling the app removes all locally stored progress. For any
  data held by RevenueCat, use their privacy controls.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal
information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the "Effective date" above will reflect the latest version.
