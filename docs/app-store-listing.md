## Description
```
Somewhere past the tree line, something is waiting to be seen.

Picross: Cryptozoology is a nonogram (picross / griddler) puzzle game wrapped in a
cryptid-hunter's field journal. Use the number clues to fill the grid one logical step at a
time — and when the last square falls into place, a hidden creature is revealed: Sasquatch,
Mothman, a Great Lakes serpent, the Fresno Nightcrawler, and hundreds more.

• 400 hand-drawn puzzles across 5 regions — the Pacific Northwest, Appalachia, the Great
  Lakes, the Desert Southwest, and the Atlantic coast.
• Four difficulty tiers, from quick 5×5 evidence icons to sprawling 25×25 capstone beasts.
• Every puzzle is uniquely solvable by pure logic — no guessing required.
• Pinch to zoom and pan the board; tap to fill, mark, and undo.
• A field-note case file for every creature you uncover.
• Two regions free to play. Unlock everything — all 5 regions, ad-free — with a single
  one-time purchase.

Cozy, tactile, and quietly atmospheric. Sharpen your logic. Fill the drawer marked OPEN.
```
