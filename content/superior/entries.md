## superior-001 · The Crossing

**Voice:** notebook
**Year:** 1968
**Credibility:** medium

Two logging roads meet north of the ore docks, and for three nights a shape stood exactly where they crossed. It did not move when the trucks slowed. By the fourth night only the tracks remained, pressed a hand's depth into dry gravel.

## superior-002 · Hollow-Eyes

**Voice:** firstPerson
**Year:** 1977
**Credibility:** low

I only saw the face, if you could call it that. Two pale holes where eyes should sit, and a mouth that did not close. It watched me from the tree line until the porch light came on, and then there was nothing there but the cold coming off the lake.

## superior-003 · The Antlered

**Voice:** victorian
**Year:** 1901
**Credibility:** medium

The guide would not speak its name above a whisper. A tall figure, crowned with the rack of a stag yet standing upright as a man, was observed at the ridge line at dusk. It regarded our party without fear, then withdrew into the birches as though it had never been.

## superior-004 · The Lake Serpent

**Voice:** deadpan
**Year:** 1983
**Credibility:** high

A dark coil, maybe forty feet, moving against the current off the point. Three witnesses, one photograph, no ripples where there should have been ripples. The ferry logged it as driftwood. Driftwood does not swim upstream.
